Chapter 18 – Current: The Keeper and the Knots
The path narrows again, twisting through dense trees, roots pulling at our feet. The light filters through leaves in shifting patterns, like an ancient code written across the ground. I move carefully, every step measured. Ash stays close, his presence steady, silent, his eyes scanning the space ahead and behind.
A faint hum drifts through the air. At first, I think it is the wind. Then I notice it is rhythmic, deliberate—like footsteps, but slower, lighter, almost floating. We approach a small clearing, and there she is.
A woman crouches beside a fire, tending it with slow, careful movements. Her hair is twisted, coiled into intricate knots that spiral down her back. Each loop and tangle seems alive, like it contains a memory. She does not look at us immediately, yet I feel her awareness, as if she senses our presence.
I hesitate. Tools are at my side, sticks and stones for balance, not for harm. Ash glances at me, a silent question. I shake my head, signaling understanding. No action but observation.
She stands, her eyes finally meeting mine. Recognition flickers, subtle but unmistakable. I feel a strange familiarity, though I do not know her. She moves slowly toward us, hands extended, palms open.
“Keeper,” I whisper inside my mind, the word forming with uncertainty. Not yet learned, but guessed. The knots in her hair, the fire, the rhythm—they speak of knowledge, of paths, of the way forward.
Ash tilts his head, silent, as I step forward. She speaks in a language I understand fully. Words I know, shaped like home, like the river and trees and the sun I grew up under. I realize then why I feel familiarity: she is from where I am from. Her knowledge is a map of what I have never walked, but somehow belongs to me.
She gestures at her hair, at the coils and twists. I understand not the word, but the meaning. Each loop, each knot, a memory, a direction, a lesson from the path. I watch, fascinated, learning without speaking.
Ash crouches beside me, studying her hands, the fire, the knots. He does not speak. I sense a question in his posture, a curiosity, and I answer in gestures, pointing to the hair, the fire, the path. He nods slowly, comprehension growing.
The Keeper smiles faintly. No words are needed yet. She begins to guide us, showing a minor twist in the path that avoids a hidden drop—a trap left by others who misuse knowledge. I watch closely, realizing the knots are not just decoration, not just tradition—they are a language, a living map.
The fire crackles. Shadows move across her face and the knots in her hair. Each flicker seems to whisper history, experience, survival. I feel the weight of what she carries, and the responsibility of what I might carry in turn.
Ash and I continue to observe, learning the rhythm of her movements, the language of the twists and coils. The path ahead seems less mysterious, yet more alive, as if it knows we are listening, watching, learning.
We leave the clearing, carrying the lessons without speaking them. The knots, the fire, the Keeper—her presence echoes within me. I understand that the path is not just forward, but shaped by those who walked it, twisted into history, memory, survival. And I know, even now, that this knowledge will protect, guide, and challenge us as the journey continues.
