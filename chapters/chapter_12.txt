Chapter 12 – Current: Shadows Among Knots
The path narrows. Trees close in, their branches intertwining like the knots in the boy’s hair. I step carefully over roots, scanning for subtle changes in the ground—uneven stones, damp soil, the faint scuff of another traveler. Ash moves beside me, steady and confident, though I notice his fingers twitch as if tracing invisible patterns in the air.
We reach a clearing where a Keeper waits, kneeling by another boy. Knotted hair, loops, and twists—each movement deliberate, meaningful. The boy’s head tilts as the Keeper adjusts a strand, her hands moving like a silent conversation with memory itself.
I crouch nearby, watching every line, every pattern. The knots tell of the paths taken, the obstacles overcome, the dangers avoided. Yet, even here, I sense what is unsaid: not all knots are truthful. Some twist with deception. A false Keeper could mislead with just a subtle change. Observation alone is not enough; intuition must follow.
Ash leans in, eyes bright with curiosity. He mimics a small twist in the boy’s hair. I shake my head subtly. Patience, careful. Learning is not mimicry—it is understanding the meaning behind the motion.
A figure steps from the shadow of the trees. Not a Keeper, not an Archivist. I feel the weight of intent immediately, though no weapon is drawn. The stranger’s eyes are cold, assessing. Ash freezes beside me. I feel the subtle pull in the air—the tension of someone who would mislead, or worse.
The stranger speaks, words I do not fully understand. Ash’s eyes flicker with recognition, but I remain cautious. Deception does not always wear a clear mask. It can hide behind courtesy, curiosity, even friendship.
The Keeper stands, her hands still on the boy’s hair, and gestures sharply. The stranger steps back, uncertain. I sense the lesson forming before it is spoken: not everyone leading the way deserves to be followed. Trust must be earned, not assumed.
We move on, stepping carefully along the path. The clearing fades behind us, the trees opening into a gentle slope. Water runs along a hidden channel, its quiet voice guiding. I glance at Ash, and he smiles faintly, the tension easing. Humor, even in caution, binds us.
The knots in the boy’s hair, the pages held by the Archivist, the subtle warning of the stranger—all form a single lesson: the path is not only physical. It is mental. Emotional. Moral.
I realize that we are learning not just to walk, but to discern. To see truth, deception, intention. To understand that not all who lead are guides, and not all who warn are enemies.
And somewhere in the back of my mind, a name forms—Valley Feeders. I do not yet know their full story, but the shadow of what they represent lingers in the edges of my awareness. A warning that even the path, carefully studied, can be twisted by greed, fear, or survival.
I tighten my pack and step forward. Ash moves with me, silent but alert. The path continues, open and narrow in turns, unpredictable, and alive. Every step is a choice. Every choice carries weight.
