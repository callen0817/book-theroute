Chapter 14 – Current: False Promises
The path narrows again, winding between low hills and sparse trees. I notice the subtle shifts in the air—the dryness, the faint copper scent of disturbed earth. Ash steps lightly, scanning the edges, his eyes catching movements I might miss.
Ahead, a small group gathers, men and women with tools in hand, beckoning travelers forward. “Farm ahead,” one calls, voice friendly but rushed. “Work, gather, return with resources.” The words sound simple, helpful.
I stop. My fingers tighten on the pack strap. Ash pauses beside me, tilting his head. He recognizes the tone, the careful construction of persuasion. I do not fully understand the words, but my mind senses the imbalance. Something is off.
We approach cautiously, keeping distance. One of them steps forward, offering water, bread. “You’ll help, you’ll be fed,” they say. The terrain slopes sharply behind them, and I see loose stones that could turn a step into disaster.
I glance at Ash. He nods slightly. Humor flickers in his eyes, though tension tightens his jaw. The lesson is clear: not every path, even when inviting, leads to life. Some paths twist with intent masked as kindness.
We move slowly, testing the ground, observing their actions. I notice a pattern in their gestures—small, almost imperceptible—directing travelers toward the edge. My stomach tightens. This is no farm. The smell of iron, faint and unsettling, rises from the valley below.
A faint memory surfaces—the twisted roots, the knots, the Keeper’s silent warning. Deception does not always shout. It whispers. It invites. It promises safety while concealing danger.
Ash glances at me, his eyes narrowing. He steps forward, speaking words I do not understand, yet I sense his meaning. The group freezes. A tension spreads, palpable, thick. Their smiles falter, their gestures halt.
We step aside, choosing a narrow path along the slope, careful with each footfall. Behind us, the group murmurs, frustrated. Their system, their control, disrupted. The Valley Feeders, I think. Their methods are not born of cruelty alone but of greed, fear, and the twisted remnants of a law gone wrong.
A faint echo of the past whispers in the rocks and roots: they once were told it was a farm, a place of provision. Now it is a tool for survival, for the few who believe themselves entitled. They do not understand that deception corrodes the foundation of life itself.
We move past, unscathed but alert. Ash laughs quietly, breaking the tension. I allow myself a small smile, relief tempered with understanding. The path is merciless, but it teaches. Every misstep, every near-fall, every observation adds to the knowledge we carry.
I glance at Ash. He nods. Partnership is survival. Observation is guidance. Intuition is power.
The path widens again. Sunlight spills over the low hills, touching the twisted roots and scattered stones. Every step is deliberate, measured, alive. And somewhere in the folds of the valley, the Valley Feeders wait, a silent reminder that deception walks among us, and only vigilance keeps the path true.
