Chapter 17 – Current: The Edge of Trust
The path narrows, jagged stones underfoot, roots twisting like veins across the dirt. Wind rises, brushing against my skin, carrying the faint tang of smoke and iron. I know the hollowed farm lies behind us, yet its shadow lingers in my chest.
Ash walks beside me, steady, silent, eyes scanning the edges of the path. He notices things I sometimes miss—the subtle shift of a stone, the faint echo of movement, the posture of travelers ahead.
A group emerges from a bend, their robes simple but stained with earth, their hands carrying tools—or what I consider tools—long enough to be mistaken for staffs. They greet us with smiles that do not reach their eyes.
“You go forward,” one says, pointing to a narrow ridge above the valley. His tone is friendly, inviting. But the way the others glance downward, toward the ledge, tells another story.
Ash shifts slightly, signaling caution. I tighten my grip on a smooth stone I carry, a tool for walking, not for fighting. My mind recalls the whispers of the valley: the Valley Feeders. They follow instructions, they follow law corrupted into survival, and the path they offer is often a path to the edge—literally.
We step lightly, testing the ground, the tools we carry offering balance. A misstep could mean falling into the hands of those who trust in the deception of order. I watch the group’s movements, gestures, the rhythm of their instructions. Each smile, each pointing hand, each step toward the edge is a question. A test.
The child we guided from the farm days ago would not survive this path, I think. Some survive by instinct, others by misfortune. Ash glances at me. He understands the logic in silence, the calculus of trust, the lessons written not in words but in observation.
We veer slightly, choosing a line farther from the edge, testing the reaction of the group. Their eyes widen, and for a moment, they hesitate. Their trust in the “law” falters as they watch us navigate on our own terms. A subtle power: presence, observation, understanding.
The ridge opens onto a plateau, sparse grass, and the wind clears. The valley stretches below, the shadows of the feeders lingering at its edges. I inhale, tasting freedom, understanding the weight of choice, of awareness.
Ash moves beside me, hand brushing against mine, a brief connection. Words are unnecessary here. The lessons are learned in steps taken carefully, in the choices made, in the observation of intent.
We continue along the path, the valley receding behind us, yet its lessons echo: trust is earned, instincts are sharpened, deception is a tool as old as the path itself. And I know, even here, even now, that the journey is only deepening.
The sun dips lower, the plateau casting long shadows, and I sense the cycles repeating. The valley feeds itself, the path continues, and we walk, side by side, carrying knowledge that is both burden and shield.
