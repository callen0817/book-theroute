Chapter 5 – Current: Moving Together
The path winds and dips, and water gathers in small pools along the ruts. My feet press into mud, leaving prints that vanish as the next step slides them away. The water moves like the path itself—constant, shaping, patient.
Ash walks ahead, silent as always. I watch the way he shifts his weight, the subtle motions of his hands. He notices the smallest details, the signs in the leaves, the way the light hits a rock, the direction of the wind. I try to imitate, but my movements are clumsy at first. He glances back once, eyes meeting mine. A small nod. I understand: follow. Watch. Learn.
We reach a shallow stream. Stones slick with moss. The current tugs at my boots. I pause. Ash steps forward without hesitation, finding a rhythm, hopping from stone to stone with ease. I study him, testing my own balance. My foot slips, water splashing, cold, sharp. I laugh, though quietly. He glances back, lips twitching. I think I see a smile.
The stream twists, wider and deeper. I hesitate. Ash waits. I study the movement, the flow, the hidden currents beneath the surface. Then I step. Carefully. Slowly. The water hisses past my calves, threatening to pull me. But I focus, breathe, move with it rather than against it. Ash meets my eyes, and I feel something shift—a wordless acknowledgment. We are moving together now, a rhythm forming, separate bodies, a shared path.
The stream opens into a small pond, the surface smooth and reflective. We stop at its edge, breathing, letting the water carry away tension, letting it mark a moment. I glance at Ash. He tilts his head toward me, questioning, not speaking, and I feel the thread again, faint but real. Understanding. Connection.
From the pond, the path climbs again. Trees close in, thick with moss and shade. Shadows mingle with sunlight, firelight of the day fading, water flowing into leaves and roots. The path here is uneven. Rocks jut, roots tangle. A false step could send us tumbling.
We pause at a clearing, noticing footprints. Someone has been here. Tools left behind. Twisted, knotted hair caught on a branch. A keeper? Perhaps. I kneel, brushing the hair from the stick, examining it. The pattern is intricate, deliberate. I do not yet know what it says, but I sense meaning. Knowledge carried in braids, in markings, in patterns left behind.
Ash watches silently. I glance at him. He tilts his head, as if saying: Do you understand? I shake my head. He shrugs, and that alone teaches me more than words could.
We continue. The path narrows further, water seeping along its edges, small streams cutting across it. I feel my awareness sharpen, senses stretching to cover both my body and the space around Ash. The world teaches in motion, and we move together, cautious but deliberate.
A gust of wind carries the scent of smoke. Another traveler? Perhaps. I grip a stick loosely, ready to defend, though I do not yet understand what that would mean. Ash steps forward, calm, trusting the flow. I follow, stepping carefully, balancing, listening. The path moves, and we move with it.
Water runs through everything—the path, our feet, the small pools, the mud, the leaves. Its sound teaches. Its rhythm guides. And I understand, in a way I cannot yet name, that this is only the beginning.
